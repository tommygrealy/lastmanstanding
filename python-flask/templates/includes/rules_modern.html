Last Man Standing: Competition Rules

Entry Fee

Pay your entry fee in Week 1. No additional payments are required afterward. All entry fees contribute to the prize pot.

Weekly Team Selection

Each week, select a team playing a match that week. You may only select each team once during the competition.

A winning selection advances you. A losing or drawn selection costs you one life; you are eliminated when you have no lives left.

Lives

Each player starts the competition with three lives. A life is lost when your selected team loses or draws. Keep playing while you have at least one life remaining.

Dynamite

Three random teams are chosen as killer teams each week and marked with 🧨. If you select one of these teams and it wins, you earn dynamite.

You can use dynamite to remove one life from another player.

Selection Deadline

Make your selection before kick-off of the first match of the game week. You can change your selection up until then by selecting “cancel prediction”.

Missed Submission

If you fail to submit a selection, the system will automatically pick a randomly chosen team from the teams still available to you.

Competition Outcome and Stalemates

The competition continues until only one player remains. That player wins the pot.

If all remaining players lose in the same round, they all advance to the next round. If this happens in two consecutive weeks, the competition resets: all players return with three lives, and all historical team selections are discarded.

If only two players remain, the competition ends and the pot is split equally if both lose more than once while they are the only two players left.
